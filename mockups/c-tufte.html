Robert Ward

Postdoctoral Scholar · Knowledge Lab, University of Chicago

rbward@uchicago.edu   cv   scholar   github

I’m interested in how complex organization, curiosity, improvisation, and creativity emerge and sustain themselves — in human systems and increasingly in AI ones. What processes and forms in an agent’s environment help collectives sustain curiosity and creativity as nearly everything else is in flux?

During my PhD I was particularly interested in how we can play with and break out of the categories and networks that organize social behavior — studying the disciplinary architecture of scientific texts, how scientists combine knowledge across established traditions, and when self-organization fails.

These days I am trying to understand how to create contexts that help human-AI collectives do the same: how language models represent concepts, when AI collectives outperform individual agents, and how to construct diverse artificial societies to explore frontier science.
