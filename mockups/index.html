Five mockups

Same content, five visual directions. Each varies along a different dimension. Side-by-side compare →

  • Current site For reference — Syne / Young Serif / cream palette / photo / news section.
  • A — Bactra (pure Shalizi) Times New Roman, white, default blue links, <hr> dividers. The literal 90s academic page. Maximum austerity.
  • B — Garamond minimal EB Garamond on warm off-white, narrow column, year-as-column lists, dark red links with subtle underline. Bookish and quiet.
  • C — Tufte (margin notes) Wide layout: years float in the left margin, co-authors/venue in the right margin. Echoes your CV's marginpar geometry.
  • D — Academic hybrid Charter serif body, sans-serif H1 and section labels, small photo at top. Closer to Tao / Aaronson / typical CS faculty pages.
  • E — One quiet accent Iowan / Palatino, near-black links with muted-green underline accent and § section markers. Almost as bare as A but keeps a thread of personality.
