Triadic Listening

May 2026

Make a playlist of three songs with minutes of silence in between.

5 minutes silence
a song
1 minute silence
b song
1 minute silence
c song
5 minutes silence

I like to choose songs that seem like they are in dialogue with one another. Usually songs I’ve heard before.

The dialogue might be evolutionary, like shifts in the interpretation of male heartbreak over time.

  • Hank Williams, I’m so lonely I could cry
  • Elvis Presley, Suspicious Minds
  • Perfume Genius, Queen

Or it could be combinatorial, an artist like Robbie Basho who integrated country blues with Indian ragas to write “zen buddhist cowboy music.” Maybe something from John Hurt and Ravi Shankar would do the trick.

It can really be anything you like.

Find a very quiet room, or good headphones. Get comfortable, start the playlist and close your eyes. Stay at least until the playlist is finished.

You can do this with friends too. But it is important that nobody speaks while they are in the room, and it helps to avoid immediately talking about it once you leave.
